# Swift_Condenser Legacy Analysis
## Research First Draft

## 1. Project Title
Swift_Condenser Legacy Analysis: Mapping Maruti Suzuki sales to market contribution for a client.

## 2. Executive Summary
This document captures the first structured draft of the research effort focused on understanding how Maruti Suzuki sales relate to broader market contribution. The intent is to provide a clear analytical baseline, outline the business question, and define the structure for a deeper analysis.

The initial draft is intentionally organized as a working document: it highlights the key problem statement, data needs, analytical approach, and likely areas of focus before the final findings are fully validated.

## 3. Business Context
The client is interested in understanding the relationship between Maruti Suzuki sales performance and the broader market contribution across relevant segments and time periods. This analysis aims to identify patterns in contribution, volume trends, and performance variation that may inform strategic decisions.

## 4. Research Objective
The primary goal of this research is to assess how Maruti Suzuki sales performance maps to market contribution and to provide an evidence-based view of demand patterns, product contribution, and market positioning.

### Key research questions
- How do Maruti Suzuki sales trends align with overall market contribution?
- Which segments or product categories contribute the most to observed performance?
- Are there identifiable patterns in growth, share, or demand concentration over time?
- What factors appear to influence contribution and performance in the relevant market context?

## 5. Scope and Boundaries
### In scope
- Historical sales performance
- Market contribution analysis
- Segment or category-level comparisons
- Pattern identification and trend analysis

### Out of scope
- Detailed financial modeling beyond the agreed analytical scope
- Competitive benchmarking beyond the defined market frame
- Operational or supply-chain diagnostics unless specifically requested

## 6. Data Sources
This draft assumes the following categories of data will be used in the analysis:

- Maruti Suzuki sales records by period
- Industry or market contribution data
- Product or segment-level volume and share information
- Any available client-specific performance inputs

### Data quality considerations
- Consistency in reporting periods and units
- Match between product categories and market segments
- Removal or flagging of anomalies and missing data
- Documentation of assumptions used in aggregation or mapping

## 7. Methodology
The first-draft methodology is designed to keep the analysis structured and traceable.

1. Define the business question and the relevant market scope.
2. Gather and validate sales and contribution data.
3. Standardize units, periods, and product/category definitions.
4. Map sales behavior to market contribution using trend and share analysis.
5. Identify key drivers, outliers, and emerging patterns.
6. Summarize findings, risks, and strategic implications.

## 8. Analytical Framework
### 8.1 Market contribution mapping
This part of the analysis focuses on comparing Maruti Suzuki sales to the broader market to determine where contribution is strongest and how it shifts over time.

### 8.2 Sales trend review
A review of historical sales movement helps identify whether stronger market contribution is a result of long-term momentum, volume expansion, seasonal effects, or product mix changes.

### 8.3 Segment and product lens
If the dataset permits, it is useful to break the analysis into segments or vehicle categories to understand where sales concentration exists and which categories drive the majority of contribution.

## 9. Preliminary Findings
This section is intentionally left as a working draft pending full validation.

### Draft observations
- Sales performance appears to be strongly tied to broader market contribution patterns.
- Long-term direction and short-term fluctuations should be reviewed separately.
- Product-level contribution may reveal more actionable insight than aggregate totals alone.
- A clear distinction between volume growth and share growth is essential.

## 10. Risks and Limitations
- Data availability and reporting consistency may affect comparability across periods.
- Aggregation level may obscure category-specific dynamics.
- External market effects may not be fully isolated from company-specific performance.
- Historical data quality may require assumptions that should be documented.

## 11. Recommended Next Steps
1. Validate all source datasets and unit definitions.
2. Build a clean, time-based dataset for analysis.
3. Segment sales by product category or relevant market split.
4. Compare sales movement against market contribution trends.
5. Finalize findings into a concise client-facing narrative.

This first draft provides a structured foundation for the research effort. It clarifies the research goal, establishes the business context, and outlines the likely analytical path needed to map Maruti Suzuki sales to market contribution in a credible and decision-oriented way.
