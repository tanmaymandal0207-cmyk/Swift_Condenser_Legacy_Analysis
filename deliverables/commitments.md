# Project Commitments

## Objective
To develop a structured analysis that maps Maruti Suzuki sales performance to market contribution in a way that is understandable, evidence-based, and decision-oriented for the client.

## Core commitments
1. Maintain a clear and agreed research question for the full duration of the project.
2. Define the relevant market scope and analytical boundaries before major analysis begins.
3. Separate broader market performance from brand-specific performance to avoid misleading conclusions.
4. Use consistent time periods, units, and category definitions across all datasets and outputs.
5. Keep the repository organized so research, analysis, and client deliverables remain traceable.
6. Maintain version control for all research drafts, reports, and revisions produced in the project.
7. Document all assumptions, exclusions, and data limitations transparently in the research record.
8. Prioritize evidence-based findings over unsupported narrative or speculative interpretation.
9. Validate data quality and completeness before finalizing conclusions or presenting them to stakeholders.
10. Review segment-level and product-level patterns before drawing strategic conclusions.
11. Ensure that the final client-ready report is concise, structured, and suitable for executive review.
12. Verify that all key findings can be linked back to a clear analytical basis or source dataset.
13. Keep the client narrative focused on business relevance, decision support, and actionability.
14. Flag any unresolved ambiguity, missing data, or sensitivity in the analysis before delivery.
15. Maintain a clear distinction between working draft material and final validated output.
16. Prepare the repository so that future contributors can continue the analysis without losing context.

## Scope commitments
- Maintain a clear research question and market definition.
- Separate broad market trends from brand-specific performance.
- Use consistent time periods and data definitions across the analysis.
- Document assumptions, risks, and limitations transparently.
- Ensure the analysis remains within the defined business objective and client brief.

## Deliverable commitments
- A first research draft with clear problem framing and analytical approach.
- A client-ready report summarizing the key findings and strategic implications.
- A repository structure that keeps research, analysis, and deliverables organized.
- Supporting documentation that explains methodology, scope, and key assumptions.
- A revision-ready set of materials for future refinement or stakeholder review.

## Quality commitments
- Prefer traceable analysis over unsupported conclusions.
- Clarify where the document is a working draft versus a validated final report.
- Keep the narrative focused on business relevance and decision support.
- Ensure the report reflects data quality checks and analytical discipline.
- Avoid overstating results when evidence is incomplete or indirect.

## Review commitments
- Validate all source data before finalizing conclusions.
- Review segment decomposition and contribution logic before external presentation.
- Ensure the final report is concise, structured, and presentation-ready.
- Confirm that all claims align with the evidence and project scope.
- Prepare final outputs for stakeholder review with clear, defensible summaries.

## Governance commitments
- Preserve a clean record of progress through repository commits and versioned files.
- Track major analytical decisions in documentation so the rationale remains visible.
- Use structured naming conventions for research, outputs, and supporting materials.
- Ensure new work is integrated in a way that does not compromise traceability.
- Maintain a review-ready project state at each major milestone.
