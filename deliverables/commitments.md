# Project Commitments

## Objective
To develop a structured analysis that maps Maruti Suzuki sales performance to market contribution in a way that is understandable, evidence-based, and decision-oriented for the client.

## Scope commitments
- Maintain a clear research question and market definition.
- Separate broad market trends from brand-specific performance.
- Use consistent time periods and data definitions across the analysis.
- Document assumptions, risks, and limitations transparently.

## Deliverable commitments
- A first research draft with clear problem framing and analytical approach.
- A client-ready report summarizing the key findings and strategic implications.
- A repository structure that keeps research, analysis, and deliverables organized.

## Quality commitments
- Prefer traceable analysis over unsupported conclusions.
- Clarify where the document is a working draft versus a validated final report.
- Keep the narrative focused on business relevance and decision support.

## Review commitments
- Validate all source data before finalizing conclusions.
- Review segment decomposition and contribution logic before external presentation.
- Ensure the final report is concise, structured, and presentation-ready.
